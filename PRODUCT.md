# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Prospective students and their families, plus potential mentor applicants and institutional partners. They visit the site to understand who 学屿教育 is, what it stands for, and whether the team feels credible, thoughtful, and worth contacting.

## Product Purpose

The site exists to communicate trust, positioning, and service philosophy for 学屿教育. Success means visitors quickly understand the brand's story, its partner ecosystem, how it works, and why it is different from a generic study-abroad agency.

## Brand Personality

Calm, credible, mentor-like. The voice should feel warm but self-possessed, with confidence earned through structure, clarity, and proof rather than sales language.

## Anti-references

Generic agency landing pages, boxy card decks with identical treatment, loud pitch copy, crowded sections with no narrative flow, and decorative UI that competes with the message.

## Design Principles

1. Lead with the story, then earn trust with proof.
2. Make the page read as one continuous argument, not separate islands of content.
3. Use spacing and hierarchy to show what matters most.
4. Keep interactions quiet, obvious, and supportive rather than attention-seeking.
5. Let partner credibility and service depth do the heavy lifting instead of visual gimmicks.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use strong text contrast, maintain clear focus states, keep layouts responsive across desktop and mobile, and avoid motion that blocks reading or comprehension.
